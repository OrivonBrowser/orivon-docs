# Get involved

Orivon is open source and built in public, with its community. You do not need to write code to
move it forward, and you do not need anyone's approval to start.

## Ways to contribute

<Cards>
  <Card title="Code">
    Orivon is TypeScript on Electron, with no compiler needed. Fix an issue, improve a feature, or
    [port an app](/docs/build/node-and-electron). Start with
    [CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/OrivonBrowser/orivon-mvp/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Use it and report">
    Daily use finds what tests miss. Send a report with **Report a problem** in the browser, or open
    an issue on GitHub.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Content and growth">
    Posts, threads, articles, videos and translations that explain what Orivon does. Share them in
    Discord's **content & ideas** channel.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Community">
    Welcome newcomers, answer questions, join events, and help run a regional channel.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Design and writing">
    Interface design, illustrations, and documentation like this page.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Partnerships">
    Projects and people in Web3 who want to build with Orivon or bring it to their audience.
  </Card>
</Cards>

Open roles change often, so they live in Discord's **roles-openings** channel rather than here.

## Start in three steps

1. **Join the [Discord](https://discord.gg/DuRg87MvgD)** and introduce yourself in
   **introductions**.
2. **Read the contributor path** in **contributor-path**: the community roles, and what earns each
   one.
3. **Start.** Contribute in the way that suits you. To propose something bigger, or to apply for a
   role, open a ticket in **open-ticket** and the team will reply there.

## The contributor path

| Role | Who it is for |
|---|---|
| Orivon Cadet | New, active supporters: in Discord, on X, sharing ideas and feedback |
| X Publisher | Regular authors of original posts about Orivon, with a private channel to the core team |
| Involved | Trusted contributors who support the project consistently and meaningfully |
| Elite | The core team: trust, responsibility and long-term contribution |

Community roles such as Contributor, Creator, Vanguard and Legend recognise activity, educational
content and long-term dedication; **roles-details** explains each one.

## How contributions are recognised

Every contribution is tracked, so none of it depends on someone remembering:

- **Code and documentation:** your commits and pull requests on
  [GitHub](https://github.com/orgs/OrivonBrowser/repositories).
- **Content:** what you share in **content & ideas**, recorded in **content-merits**.
- **Community growth:** recorded in **growth-merits**.
- **Anything else:** post a self-report in **contrib-reports**. A spreadsheet you keep up to date
  works best; the channel's pinned message has a template and the current priorities. A ✅ from the
  team means it has been recorded.

Each week the team picks a **Contributor Spotlight**: the people whose work made the clearest
difference, with the reason stated publicly.

## Core team and partnerships

The core team is made of contributors who are **committed** (working actively with the rest of the
team), **trusted** (built over time) and **coordinated** (a shared way of working and compatible
hours). To be considered, fill in the
[core team and partnership form](https://forms.gle/pz8cBxYn7P8CL3pM8) and open a ticket.

Developers can apply through the
[developer form](https://forms.gle/PL8yqbupr8YTwd1q8).

Partnerships work the same way, for projects, creators and people with reach in Web3. They give
direct access to the core team without the core-team criteria above.
